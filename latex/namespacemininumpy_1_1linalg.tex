mininumpy.\+linalg.\+inv (\begin{DoxyParamCaption}\item[{}]{a}{}\end{DoxyParamCaption})}



Calculates the inverse of a square 2D array. 

The inverse is calculated using the formula\+: inv(\+A) = (1/det(A)) \texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*} adj(\+A), where adj(\+A) is the adjugate matrix (transpose of the cofactor matrix).
